Privacy policy

A plain-language summary of the key privacy themes covered by ReferredBy.

ReferredBy uses personal and account information to create and manage profiles, verify identity, provide loan-related services and support customers.

What information is collected?

  • Personal registration information needed to create and manage a ReferredBy profile.
  • KYC and document information required for verification.
  • Transaction and communication information used to deliver the service and support customers.

How is it used?

  • To register the user, operate the profile and provide loan-related services.
  • To verify identity, reduce fraud and support compliance requirements.
  • To improve services and handle customer support interactions.

How is it protected?

  • Access controls, encrypted services and identity verification measures help protect user data.
  • Access is restricted to authorised staff, contractors and agents who need the information for service delivery.

User responsibilities

  • Keep profile access details confidential.
  • Update personal information when it changes.
  • Report any suspected compromise of account access.
